# Chris Rader

I've spent my career around builders and building: research, users, QA, and product teams. I love solving people's problems, and there are a lot of ways to do it. Lately I solve a lot of them by building with AI agents.

Day job: VP of Product Marketing at Centercode, a customer validation company. This account is what I build on my own time.

## What's here

- **[baseball-swing-analysis](https://github.com/rader-ai/baseball-swing-analysis)**: grades a baseball swing from phone video (head, posture, balance, stride) and gives one drill to work on.
- **[exit-velo-eval](https://github.com/rader-ai/exit-velo-eval)**: estimates exit velocity from phone video. When the video isn't good enough, it says "no clean read" instead of guessing.
- **[sales-call-review](https://github.com/rader-ai/sales-call-review)**: turns sales call transcripts into account timelines and a coaching report, with every quote checked against the transcript.
- **[Recording-Atlas](https://github.com/rader-ai/Recording-Atlas)**: turns a pile of recordings into a searchable, timestamped archive.
- **[youth-baseball-league-benchmarks](https://github.com/rader-ai/youth-baseball-league-benchmarks)**: fees and sponsorship data from 37 Little Leagues, so a volunteer board can see where it stands.

The first two power **SwingDino** ([swingdino.com](https://swingdino.com)), a hitting app for kids that grew out of coaching. It's in beta.

## How I build

Claude Code writes most of the code. I set the scope, write the rules, review the work, and decide when it's done. I try to say plainly what's been tested and what hasn't. The SwingDino engines each have a STORY.md with the honest version.
